# Keeping Your Furry Friends in Mind

As a pet owner, you’re not just keeping a home clean—you’re also making sure it’s safe for every family member, including the furry ones. Let’s dive into what makes pet-friendly cleaning unique and why our eco-conscious approach is a great match for pet lovers.

## Why Pet-Safe Cleaning Matters

Pets, whether cats, dogs, or even the tiniest of hamsters, are naturally curious and will explore every nook and cranny. This includes sniffing, pawing, or licking areas we’ve just cleaned. The trouble is that many standard cleaning products, like bleach, ammonia, and phenols, can irritate sensitive areas like eyes, skin, and noses. And if they ingest or inhale even small amounts of these chemicals, it could lead to nausea, respiratory discomfort, or more serious health issues over time.

Keeping pets safe from these potential hazards isn’t just about the occasional spill; it’s also about choosing products and practices that are safe for them in the long run.

## Smile Cleaning’s Eco-Friendly Approach

At Smile Cleaning, we’re committed to using products that are both tough on grime and gentle on the environment—and by extension, gentle on your pets. This means we select plant-based, natural cleaners, such as vinegar and enzyme-based solutions, whenever possible. These types of products break down naturally, leaving fewer residues behind that could affect your pets if they wander through a recently cleaned room.

With our approach, you can feel confident knowing that our cleaning methods are not just effective but also safe for your entire household, especially the four-legged members.

## Pet-Friendly Cleaning Practices You Can Use at Home

To further ensure your pets’ safety, here are some easy steps you can take when cleaning up at home:

- **Keep Pets Out of the Room**: Whether we’re cleaning for you or you’re taking it on yourself, keep pets out of the room while cleaning and allow surfaces to dry completely before letting them back in. This minimises their contact with any lingering cleaner residue or accidental spills.

- **Rinse After Cleaning**: After cleaning surfaces where pets spend a lot of time, like floors or countertops, consider going over them with a clean, damp cloth or a quick water rinse. This extra step helps remove any leftover traces of cleaning agents.

- **Choose Natural Alternatives**: If you’re tackling a small clean-up yourself, use simple and safe household items like diluted white vinegar for floors or baking soda for removing odours. These options are not only pet-safe but also effective for everyday cleaning tasks.

- **Secure Cleaning Products Out of Reach**: Pets can be quite resourceful when it comes to investigating new smells. Store all cleaning products securely in cupboards or on high shelves, out of reach of inquisitive noses and paws.

## Common Pet Concerns—and How We Address Them

Pet owners often worry about issues like skin sensitivity, respiratory distress, or digestive upset from accidental ingestion. While cats are especially sensitive to certain chemicals, even large dogs can be affected if they walk on freshly cleaned floors or drink from a mop bucket left out by mistake.

To address these concerns, Smile Cleaning emphasises both prevention and care. We actively avoid harsh chemicals and prioritise natural alternatives, and our team is always mindful of rinsing and drying procedures in pet areas. We also focus on ventilation to reduce any lingering odours that could be uncomfortable for sensitive noses.
