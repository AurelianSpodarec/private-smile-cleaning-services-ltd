# What to Expect from Our Service

When booking a cleaning service, it’s helpful to know exactly what to expect in terms of supplies and equipment. Smile Cleaning believes in transparency and professionalism, and we’re here to clarify our approach so you can be well-prepared for your service appointment. Here’s what you need to know about our supplies and equipment policy, as well as how we’re committed to quality and eco-friendly cleaning solutions.

## Eco-Friendly Cleaning Products Available on Request

For clients who prefer eco-conscious options, Smile Cleaning offers the use of eco-friendly cleaning products. These products are available upon request for a small, flat fee of £6. The products we use are carefully selected to ensure they’re safe for both your home and the environment, helping to reduce the number of harsh chemicals introduced into your space.

This option allows you to enjoy a sustainable cleaning service without sacrificing the quality of the clean. If you choose this option, our team will arrive fully prepared with a suite of eco-friendly products to handle all regular cleaning tasks, ensuring a sparkling finish that’s gentle on surfaces and safer for the environment.

Our commitment to eco-friendly practices reflects our understanding that many clients want to make more environmentally conscious choices. If you’d like to add eco-friendly products to your service, simply let us know in advance, and we’ll take care of the rest.

## Using Your Cleaning Appliances

In line with our goal to provide a straightforward and hassle-free service, Smile Cleaning uses the household cleaning appliances you already have at home, including items like vacuums, mops, and brooms. This approach helps in two ways:

- **Reliability:** Using your appliances ensures consistency, as our team will be using equipment that’s already suited to your home’s specific needs. You can trust that each clean is as thorough as possible, without the potential variations that can come with different equipment.

- **Simplicity:** By relying on your appliances, we can travel lighter and avoid unnecessary transport of bulky equipment. This not only makes our visits smoother but also supports eco-friendly practices by reducing the overall energy and resources required to move equipment between clients.

Rest assured, our team is trained to handle various types of household appliances, and they’ll use your equipment efficiently to produce the best results. If you have specific preferences or guidelines for using your appliances, just let us know, and we’ll be happy to accommodate.

## Providing Specialised Equipment for Deep Cleans

Certain cleaning tasks, especially during deep cleans, require specialised equipment to achieve an optimal finish. Smile Cleaning provides this equipment as needed, ensuring your home receives the attention it deserves in those more detailed areas.

Our specialised tools are designed to handle tasks such as carpet and upholstery cleaning, thorough kitchen and bathroom sanitisation, and tackling high-traffic areas or other hard-to-reach spots. For example, a carpet cleaning machine may be used for tough stains, or a steam cleaner might be brought along to sanitise and refresh surfaces in your bathroom or kitchen.

By offering this service as part of our deep cleans, we make sure that every part of your home can look its best, without you needing to invest in any extra equipment or tools. You can book a deep clean with confidence, knowing that our team is equipped to handle everything from routine maintenance to detailed, specialised tasks.

## Why Choose Smile Cleaning?

At Smile Cleaning, we prioritise quality, reliability, and a commitment to eco-friendly options for our clients. Our professional approach to supplies and equipment is just one of the ways we work to offer a convenient, high-quality service every time.

Whether you’re seeking routine maintenance or an intensive deep clean, Smile Cleaning brings the expertise, care, and eco-conscious choices you can rely on. Choosing us means choosing a team dedicated to excellence and environmental awareness. We believe in providing a flexible, efficient service that meets your needs and ensures a spotless home.
