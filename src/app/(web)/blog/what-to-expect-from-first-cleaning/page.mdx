# A Guide for First-Time Clients

If you're new to professional cleaning services, we’re thrilled to welcome you to Smile Cleaning! We know that inviting a cleaner into your home is a personal decision, and we’re here to make the experience as easy and beneficial as possible. In this guide, we’ll walk you through what to expect from your first appointment and why a **Deep-Clean** is the best way to start. Plus, we’ll explain how our Smile Cleaner will work with you to make the most of the time booked and help you plan for future sessions.

## The First Appointment: How It Works

When our Smile Cleaner arrives, they’ll come fully prepared to get your home looking its best. We offer the option to use our high-quality, eco-friendly cleaning products if you’ve requested them during booking. Otherwise, we’re happy to use the products you prefer and have available. For appliances like vacuum cleaners or mops, we’ll use those you have at home, ensuring we’re working with equipment you’re comfortable with—except for the specialised tools we bring for a **Deep-Clean**.

## Assessment and Customisation

At the start of your session, our Smile Cleaner will conduct a quick assessment to ensure your booked time aligns with the work needed. This transparent step allows us to clearly indicate what can and cannot be achieved within the session. If we find that your cleaning goals exceed the time available, we’ll let you know and suggest whether more or less time might be required for future bookings. This way, you’re fully informed, and we can tailor our service to suit your needs perfectly.

## Why Start with a Deep-Clean?

For your first cleaning, we highly recommend opting for a **Deep-Clean**. This intensive service allows us to reach into overlooked spots and tackle hidden dust and grime that might have built up over time. A **Deep-Clean** involves detail-oriented work, covering everything from skirting boards and cupboard tops to behind appliances and other hard-to-reach areas. We bring specialised tools for these tasks, ensuring a thorough result that sets a clean foundation for easier upkeep in future sessions. It also improves your home’s overall air quality—a great benefit for households with young children or pets.

## The Smile Cleaning Process

To ensure quality and consistency, Smile Cleaning follows a structured process for every clean:

- **Initial Review**: Our cleaner arrives and confirms any specific instructions or priority areas with you, setting clear expectations from the start.
- **Detailed Cleaning**: Whether using our products or yours, we tackle each room systematically, focusing on high-traffic areas and dust-prone spots. For Deep-Cleans, we utilise additional tools to achieve a comprehensive clean.
- **Progress Check-In**: If you’re home, our cleaner will provide updates as they work, allowing for any adjustments and keeping you informed.
- **Final Review and Recommendations**: Before wrapping up, we’ll walk through the completed areas with you to ensure satisfaction. We’ll also discuss if future sessions might benefit from adjusted timings based on the work done.

## Setting Up for Success with Future Appointments

Your first cleaning session helps us get acquainted with your space and needs. If any adjustments are necessary for future bookings, we’ll communicate these clearly. Starting with a **Deep-Clean** establishes a solid base, making regular maintenance more efficient and tailored to your home's specific requirements.

## Why Choose Smile Cleaning?

At Smile Cleaning, our focus extends beyond just cleaning. We’re dedicated to enhancing your home’s comfort and ensuring you feel supported throughout the process. Our commitment to eco-friendly products and sustainable practices means your home benefits from a green approach to cleanliness, whether we're using our supplies or yours.

## Ready to Get Started?

Whether you’re looking for a one-off **Deep-Clean** or regular maintenance, Smile Cleaning is here to make your home shine. Book your first session with us today and experience the difference of a customised, thorough clean designed to fit your space and lifestyle.
