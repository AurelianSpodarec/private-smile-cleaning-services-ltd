# Tips to Streamline Your Ironing Routine

Ironing can often feel like a chore we all dread, but it doesn’t have to be! With a few practical tips, you can transform this task into a breeze. Let’s explore some strategies that will make ironing not just bearable, but almost enjoyable!

## Organise Your Clothes

Before you even plug in the iron, take a moment to sort your clothes. Group them by fabric type and care instructions. This speeds up the process and helps you avoid mishaps, like scorching delicate fabrics.

Keep all your cottons together, as they usually require a higher heat, and separate your synthetics and delicates. Sorting them by colour can help, too! If you’re feeling extra organised, fold them or hang them up in the order you’ll be ironing them.

## Use the Right Heat Settings

Every fabric has its sweet spot when it comes to temperature. Always check the care label for guidance. Start with the lowest setting and work your way up if needed. This prevents damage and ensures your clothes look their best.

Start with the synthetics first, as they require lower temperatures, then move on to the cotton and linen items that can take the heat. This way, you won’t have to keep changing settings on your iron.

## Invest in Quality Tools

A good iron can make a world of difference. Look for one with adjustable steam settings, a smooth soleplate, and a comfortable grip. If you want to cut down on time, consider a steam generator or a garment steamer for quick touch-ups.

Investing in a sturdy ironing board with adjustable height settings is essential. You want to work comfortably without straining your back. Trust me; having the right tools can significantly reduce your ironing time!

## Ironing Board Positioning

Set up your ironing board in a space that allows for easy movement. This makes it simpler to navigate between items, reducing the time you spend switching clothes. Ideally, you want your board in a well-lit area, so you can easily spot any creases.

If possible, position your ironing board near a power outlet to avoid stretching cords. Make sure you have enough space to manoeuvre and that you’re not knocking over anything while you iron.

## Maintain Your Equipment

Keep your iron clean and well-maintained. Regularly descaling it prevents build-up that can lead to inefficient ironing. If you notice any stains on the soleplate, wipe it down with a damp cloth and a little baking soda.

Always empty the water reservoir after use. Leaving water in the iron can lead to mineral build-up and make it less effective the next time you use it.

## Iron in a Relaxed State

Ironing can be therapeutic if you approach it with the right mindset. Put on some music or a podcast that you love. This can make the task feel less like a chore and more like a relaxing break in your day. You could even invite a friend over to chat while you both iron!

## Tackle Items in a Smart Order

Ironing isn’t just about random garments; there’s a method to it! Start with items that take the least time, like lightweight shirts, then move to heavier items like jeans or towels. This approach keeps your momentum going.

Consider working from the top down. Iron the tops first, followed by trousers, then heavier items. This strategy prevents any wrinkles from falling onto freshly ironed clothes.

## Steam, Don’t Just Iron

Using steam can really cut down your ironing time. If your iron has a steam function, use it! The steam helps to relax the fabric fibres, making it easier to smooth out wrinkles. For stubborn creases, hold the iron vertically and use the steam burst feature.

If you don’t have a steam iron, a handheld steamer can also do wonders, especially for delicate fabrics and quick touch-ups.

## Create a Comfortable Space

If you’re going to be ironing for a while, make sure you’re comfortable. Wear something relaxed, and if you can, set up a little drink station nearby with water or tea. Staying hydrated is key! Maybe treat yourself to a snack while you iron—it’s about making the experience enjoyable.

## Practice Makes Perfect

The more you iron, the better and quicker you’ll get at it. Regularly maintaining your clothes will help you develop a rhythm. Set a schedule for your ironing, whether it’s every Sunday evening or mid-week touch-ups. The more consistent you are, the less of a chore it will feel.
