# Make the Most of Your Ironing Session

Ironing can be one of those tasks that pile up until it feels overwhelming – especially if you have a busy schedule. **Smile Cleaning** is here to change that with our professional ironing services, allowing you to achieve that freshly pressed look in just 30 minutes. ## So, What Can Be Ironed in 30 Minutes?

In 30 minutes, you’ve got options. Our skilled ironing professionals can typically handle:

- **Five to Six Shirts** – Ideal if you’re looking to get work shirts or blouses pressed and ready for the week. Our team focuses on the details like collars, cuffs, and button areas, ensuring a crisp, polished finish.

  OR

- **Three Pairs of Trousers** – Perfect for work or special occasions. We’ll press the creases and ensure the fabric looks smooth, so you’re ready for any event or office meeting.

  OR

- **Light Linens** – If your home linens are looking a bit crinkled, we can manage about four smaller linen items, such as pillowcases, tea towels, or napkins, in a single session.

These options are designed to give you flexibility, letting you prioritise the items that matter most. So whether it’s shirts for the office, trousers for an outing, or linens to refresh your home, you can make the most of your session.

## How We Maximise Every Minute

Our professionals know their way around an ironing board! They’ve perfected efficient ironing techniques that allow them to work quickly without compromising on quality. Here’s how they achieve this:

- **Focus on Key Areas**: For shirts, our team gives special attention to collars, cuffs, and button lines to ensure these key areas look their best. For trousers, we make sure creases are sharp and the overall fabric is smooth.

- **Smooth Workflow**: Each ironing session is planned to avoid wasted time. Our team knows how to work through each item in a way that’s methodical, meaning they maximise each second to deliver that fresh-pressed look.
