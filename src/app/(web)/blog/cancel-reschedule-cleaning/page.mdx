## Your Flexibility Matters

At Smile Cleaning, we know that life can be unpredictable. Whether it’s a sudden change in plans or just the need for a little more flexibility, we’ve made sure that cancelling or rescheduling your cleaning appointment is as simple and stress-free as possible. With our easy-to-use **Manage Bookings** feature on the Smile Cleaning website, you have full control over your bookings, allowing you to adjust, update, or cancel appointments with just a few clicks. Read on for a step-by-step guide on how to manage your booking, and rest assured, we’re here to make it as smooth as possible.

---

### Step-by-Step Guide to Cancelling or Rescheduling

#### Log into Your Smile Cleaning Account

To get started, head over to the Smile Cleaning website and log in to your account. Once you’re logged in, you’ll find everything you need in the **Manage Bookings** section. This is your go-to spot for viewing, updating, and managing all your appointments with us.

#### Find Your Booking

Next, locate the specific booking you want to adjust. Whether it’s a one-off deep clean or your regular weekly service, all your upcoming bookings are conveniently listed here.

#### Choose Your Option: Edit, Reschedule, or Cancel

- **Edit Your Booking**: Need to add an extra service or adjust what’s on the to-do list? No problem! You can update your booking’s services and extras until 5 pm the day before your appointment. After this cutoff, changes will need to be made by either cancelling and rebooking or by rescheduling.

- **Reschedule Your Appointment**: If you need to move your appointment to a different date or time, you can do so at any time with just a few clicks. As long as you give us at least 48 hours’ notice, there won’t be any extra charges. If you need to reschedule with less than 48 hours’ notice, we apply a small £30 fee.

- **Cancel Your Appointment**: Sometimes plans change entirely, and cancelling may be the best option. You can cancel any booking, but if you do so within 48 hours or more of your scheduled appointment, there’s no fee at all! For cancellations within 48 hours, a £30 fee will apply, just to cover our team’s time and arrangements.

### More FAQs About Our Cancellation and Rescheduling Policies

- **Can I Make Changes to My Appointment on the Same Day?**
  Currently, changes to services and extras can only be made up until 5 pm the day before. For last-minute changes, we suggest rescheduling or cancelling and rebooking through the Manage Bookings feature, where the usual cancellation fees will apply.

- **What if I Have an Emergency?**
  We understand that emergencies happen! If you’re ever in a bind, feel free to reach out to our support team. While we do our best to stick to the policy for consistency, we’re always happy to help find the best solution possible for your situation.

- **Do I Need an Account to Manage My Booking?**
  Yes, you’ll need to log in to your account on the Smile Cleaning website to access the Manage Bookings feature. This ensures that all changes are secure and accurately applied to your booking.
