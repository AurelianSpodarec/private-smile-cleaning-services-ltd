# What to Do Before We Arrive

We know life can be busy, so when our Smile Cleaning team arrives, we want to make your experience as smooth and stress-free as possible! Preparing your space with just a few quick steps can help our cleaners work more efficiently, giving you the best results and leaving your home looking pristine.

Here's a handy checklist to make the most of your cleaning service:

## Tidy Up Personal Belongings

While it’s not necessary to do any cleaning beforehand, putting away personal items, such as toys, clothing, or mail, can really help our team focus on what we do best—cleaning! A clear space allows us to reach all surfaces, so feel free to store away any clutter or personal items in a drawer or cupboard.

## Ensure Clear Access to Rooms

If any rooms are particularly important to clean, ensure they’re easily accessible. Open doors to the rooms you’d like us to focus on, and let us know if any areas need extra care or should be skipped for the day. This helps us navigate quickly and gives you more control over which areas get attention.

## Remove Fragile Items

Our cleaners are careful, but accidents can happen. We recommend moving fragile or delicate items to a secure location to avoid any mishaps. This could include things like decorative items, small glassware, or collectibles that are easy to put aside for the day.

## Make Space for Cleaning Supplies

Our cleaners bring all the essentials, but it helps if there’s space to set down cleaning materials. Clearing a small area in the kitchen or hallway can make it easy for our team to organise their supplies and get started right away, saving time and making the process smoother.

## Consider Your Pets

For everyone’s comfort, it’s helpful to ensure pets are in a safe, separate area while we clean. This ensures that our team can move about freely and that your pets are not disturbed by any noise from vacuums or cleaning equipment. Plus, it keeps your furry friends happy and stress-free!

## Give Your Appliances a Quick Check

If your service includes appliance cleaning, a quick look at your fridge, oven, or microwave before we arrive can help. Emptying any perishables from the fridge or removing cookware from the oven means we can jump right into cleaning. For extra peace of mind, you might even leave us a small note for any specific cleaning instructions you’d like us to follow!

## Note Any Special Requests

At Smile Cleaning, we’re all about making your home sparkle just the way you like it! If you have any special instructions or areas that need extra attention (like a particular spot on the carpet or a favourite countertop), feel free to let us know. You can leave a little note, or tell us directly when we arrive.

## Check for Supplies You Prefer

While we bring eco-friendly cleaning products, some clients prefer certain products for specific surfaces. If you have a favourite polish, disinfectant, or cleaning cloth, simply leave it out with a quick note, and we’ll be happy to use it.

## Put Out Fresh Linens or Towels

If part of your service includes linen changing or bathroom cleaning, you can place fresh linens, towels, or bedding out for us to use. This small step helps us make your home as welcoming as possible, with beautifully prepared spaces for you to enjoy after your clean. Setting these items in a visible area, such as on the bed or bathroom counter, makes it easy for our team to incorporate them into your clean.

## Consider Any Routines or Preferences for Room Use

Some families have specific routines or preferences for room use, such as keeping windows open for fresh air or keeping certain doors closed to limit dust. If there’s anything in your routine that you’d like us to consider, we’re here to accommodate! Just let us know before we start, or leave a note, and we’ll follow any specific instructions to make the clean as tailored as possible to your home environment.

## Your Cleaning Day, Made Simple!

Taking these small steps ahead of time can really make a difference in your cleaning experience, allowing us to work efficiently and deliver the best results. We’re thrilled to be part of making your home a relaxing, clean space. Sit back, relax, and let us handle the rest—your Smile Cleaning team will take care of the details.
