## 1. Introduction
At Smile Cleaning Limited ("we," "our", “us” or "Smile Cleaning"), we are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our website (www.smile.cleaning) and our services. We adhere to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws.

## 2. How We Collect Your Information
At Smile Cleaning, we prioritise transparency and safeguarding your privacy. Here's how we collect your information:

- **Interaction with Our Platform:** When you engage with our website or mobile application, we may collect data to enhance your experience and provide tailored services.
- **Booking and Account Creation:** Your data is gathered when you make a booking or create an account, ensuring seamless service delivery and personalised experiences.
- **Account Management:** Any modifications made to your account settings or preferences are recorded to ensure our services align with your needs.
- **Feedback Collection:** Your feedback is invaluable to us. We collect data provided during feedback submissions to improve our services and enhance user satisfaction.
- **Third-Party Sources:** We may obtain technical information from select third-party sources, such as analytics providers and advertising platforms, to optimise our operations and offer relevant services.

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Information You Provide to Us
- **Contact Information:** Name, email address, phone number, and postal address.
- **Service Information:** Details about the cleaning services you request, including specific instructions about your home.
- **Payment Information:** Credit/debit card information or other payment details.
- **Feedback and Correspondence:** Information you provide when you contact us with questions, feedback, or for customer support.

### 3.2 Information We Collect Automatically
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies:** We use cookies to track your usage of our website and to personalise your experience.
- **Log Data:** Information such as your IP address, browser type, access times, pages viewed, and the referring website.

### 3.3 Information from Third Parties
- **Service Providers:** Information from third-party service providers that assist us in providing our services, such as payment processors.
- **Publicly Available Sources:** Information from publicly accessible sources.

## 8. Your Rights
Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:
- **Access:** The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectification:** The right to correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure:** The right to request the deletion of your data under certain conditions.
- **Restriction:** The right to restrict the processing of your data under certain circumstances.
- **Data Portability:** The right to receive your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format and to transmit it to another controller.
- **Objection:** The right to object to the processing of your data for direct marketing and other purposes.
- **Withdraw Consent:** The right to withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise your rights, please contact us at [privacy@smile.cleaning](mailto:privacy@smile.cleaning).
