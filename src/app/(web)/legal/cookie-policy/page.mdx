Welcome to Smile Cleaning Limited ("we," "our," "us," or "Smile Cleaning"). This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies on our website (www.smile.cleaning). By continuing to use our website, you consent to the use of cookies as outlined in this policy.

## 2. What are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They serve various purposes, such as enhancing your browsing experience, remembering your preferences, and providing personalised content.

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

### 3.1 Essential Cookies
These cookies are crucial for the basic operation of our website. They enable core functionalities like security, authentication, and accessibility. Without these cookies, certain services you have asked for cannot be provided.

### 3.2 Analytical/Performance Cookies
These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting information such as which pages are visited most frequently and any error messages encountered. This data is used to improve the performance and usability of our website.

### 3.3 Functionality Cookies
Functionality cookies remember choices you make, such as your language preferences or login details. This enables us to offer enhanced and personalised features, improving your user experience.

### 3.4 Targeting/Advertising Cookies
These cookies track your browsing habits and are used to deliver relevant advertisements to you. They may also be used by third-party advertisers to measure the effectiveness of their campaigns. These cookies collect information about your visits to our site and other sites to provide you with targeted ads.

### 3.5 Social Media Cookies
Social media cookies allow you to share content from our site with your social networks and help link activity between our website and your social media accounts. These cookies are set by social media platforms and are often linked to site functionality provided by those third parties.

## 4. How We Use Cookies
We use cookies for various purposes, including:
- **Service Provision and Personalisation:** To deliver and personalise our services according to your preferences and previous interactions.
- **Performance Analysis:** To analyse website traffic, usage patterns, and gather insights to enhance the performance and usability of our website.
- **Functionality Improvement:** To remember your choices and settings to provide a more personalised experience.
- **Advertising:** To deliver targeted advertisements based on your interests and online behaviour.
- **Security Enhancement:** To improve the security of our website and protect against fraudulent activities.

## 5. Your Cookie Choices
Most web browsers allow you to manage your cookie preferences through their settings. You can configure your browser to accept, reject, or delete cookies. Here’s how you can manage your cookie settings:

- **Browser Settings:** Adjust your browser settings to manage cookies. Instructions for managing cookies in popular browsers:
  - **Google Chrome:** Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
  - **Mozilla Firefox:** Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  - **Safari:** Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data
  - **Microsoft Edge:** Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies and site data
- **Opt-Out Tools:** Use opt-out tools provided by third-party services to manage the use of targeting/advertising cookies. For example, the UK-based YourOnlineChoices ([www.youronlinechoices.com/uk/](https://www.youronlinechoices.com/uk/)) offers a tool for opting out of interest-based advertising.

Please note that blocking or deleting cookies may affect your browsing experience and limit the functionality of certain website features.
